Borders and light, not shadows.

Resting surfaces are flat and separated by hairlines. Shadows exist only at overlay tiers, in
mist-black at low alpha — atmosphere, not drop-shadow graphics.

## Shadows

```monokit-scale elevation.shadows
```

Dark mode keeps the same tokens and expects them to read as near-invisible: a dark overlay is
distinguishable because it is a **lighter surface with a lit edge**. Never compensate.

Inner shadows are not part of the system. Buttons, checkboxes, tabs, inputs, and key caps are all
flat.
