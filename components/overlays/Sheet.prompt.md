Sheet is how a native screen asks one question — the country picker, a confirm, a short menu.

```jsx
<Sheet open={pickerOpen} onClose={() => setPickerOpen(false)} title="Select country">
  <ListGroup>
    {countries.map(c => <ListRow key={c.code} title={c.name} value={c.dial} onPress={() => pick(c)} />)}
  </ListGroup>
</Sheet>
```

Drag it down past 30% of its height, or release above 700 px/s, and it dismisses; upward drag rubber-bands, and a drag released under the threshold returns on the spatial spring leaving no trace. Both thresholds are the specification's. It enters on the emphasis pairing and exits one step shorter. It is 26px radius on the top corners only, and it is the one place in this system that carries a shadow. Do not use a centred dialog on a phone screen.
